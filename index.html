The Item System

Inscripted features a living, dynamic item ecosystem built on deterministic progression rather than pure randomness. Every piece of equipment tells a story and enables unique playstyles.

You Are What You Wear

Characters are defined by their equipment, not fixed classes. This philosophy creates diverse PvP experiences where each playstyle has strategic purpose and counter-play opportunities.

⚒️

Craftables

Modifiable items whose inscriptions (magical properties) can be altered using currency like Orbs. Craft your perfect build through deterministic progression.

  • Customizable inscriptions
  • Deterministic progression
  • Build-focused itemization
  • Orb-based modification
✨

Relics

Unique, unchangeable items designed to create unconventional builds or subvert core game mechanics. Discover powerful artifacts that define entire playstyles.

  • Unchangeable properties
  • Unique mechanics
  • Build-defining effects
  • Strategic diversity

The Archetype System

Three core attributes form the foundation of all character builds. Master the trinity of Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence to forge your perfect warrior.

🔥

Strength

Fire Element

Physical damage and mitigation. The path of raw power and unyielding defense.

⚡

Dexterity

Lightning Element

Evasiveness and mobility. Strike fast, strike true, never be where they expect.

❄️

Intelligence

Cold Element

Elemental damage and stamina. Harness the arcane forces to devastate your foes.

Choose Your Path

Marauder

Strength

The embodiment of raw power and resilience. Marauders don heavy plating armor and wield massive axes, dominating the battlefield through sheer force and unbreakable defense.

Primary Role Tank / Bruiser
Equipment Heavy Plating, Axes
Playstyle Physical Dominance

Gladiator

Strength Dexterity

Masters of balanced combat, Gladiators blend the power of Strength with the finesse of Dexterity. Wielding swords and carved plating armor, they excel at sustained damage and critical strikes.

Primary Role Duelist / DPS
Equipment Carved Plating, Swords
Playstyle Balanced Offense

Templar

Strength Intelligence

Versatile warriors who bridge the physical and arcane. Templars can emphasize raw physical prowess or devastating elemental channeling through strategic item selection.

Primary Role Spellblade / Hybrid
Equipment Adaptive Gear
Playstyle Versatile Mastery
